Should we move from Alberta to Ontario?

A wealth comparison over 25 years across different jobs and scenarios. Move the year slider to see how wealth grows over time.
The decision in a nutshell: Staying in Alberta means I keep earning through my own clinic with the NPPCP government program — more money builds up in cash because I'm running my own business. Moving to Ontario means working as a hospital/clinic NP for a salary, with a guaranteed government-style pension (HOOPP/OMERS), but less cash builds up because more is taken in taxes and pension deductions.

The trade-off: Ontario gives us family proximity (your sister, my dad, your parents) and a guaranteed pension. Alberta gives us substantially more accessible wealth over time. These charts quantify the financial side of that trade.

What the 4 colors mean (read this first)

Dark Green — Cash savings
Money in our bank account. Fully accessible anytime.
Light Green — Corp investments
Money saved inside my professional corporation. Accessible but with some tax cost to withdraw to personal account.
Orange — Home equity
The value built up in our house. Need to sell or take a home equity loan to access.
Gray — Pension (locked until ~65)
Future retirement income from HOOPP/OMERS in Ontario jobs. Cannot be touched until retirement age. Shown as present-day value.
👉 The two greens combined = "Truly Liquid" wealth. This is money we can actually use for emergencies, kids' education, businesses, or just life. The orange and gray are real wealth too, but locked up in different ways.
